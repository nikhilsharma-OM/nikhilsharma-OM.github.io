This study explores the use of AI-enabled curbside feedback — a novel approach that delivers personalized, non-punitive nudges to households — to improve recycling quality. Testing this intervention matters because AI-enabled feedback can be scaled to the household level, unlike existing interventions that require manual inspection of recycling carts and are limited to selected households.

In a 48-week field experiment across a Midwest community, we used AI-based smart recycling trucks to audit recycling carts for contamination and administer two types of green nudges — "moral suasion" and "pure" (purely informational) — to 7,600 households. We find that the AI-enabled nudges meaningfully reduced contamination and improved participation, and that moral-suasion nudges were more effective than purely informational ones.
