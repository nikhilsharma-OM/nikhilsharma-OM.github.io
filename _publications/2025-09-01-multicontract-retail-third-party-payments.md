This project extends the behavioral inquiry of Chapter 2 to a challenge that comes up frequently in conversations with retailers: managing inventory under multiple, simultaneous third-party payment contracts, where a retailer may earn low or negative profitability on one contract but must keep stocking the product because of other existing contracts for the same item. The goal is to develop prescriptive solutions applicable across contexts beyond retail pharmacy, such as food benefit programs.
