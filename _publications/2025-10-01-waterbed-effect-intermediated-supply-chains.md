Building on a five-state Medicaid claims panel spanning 2017-2025, this project explores whether regulatory actions targeting Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) practices in one state also produce spillover effects on PBM-managed plans in other states, with implications for patient access and care across state lines.
