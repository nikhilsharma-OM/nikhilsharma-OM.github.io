Focusing on evidence from metformin drug quality, this study explores whether meaningful quality differences exist among generic drugs and whether the market rewards those differences. Using FDA drug manufacturing facility inspection data, we find that inspection outcomes do not predict a drug's market price or reliably predict its sales volume, and that manufacturing quality varies by country of origin and is typically not a primary factor rewarded by the market.
