This integrative review develops a conceptual framework on supply chain intermediaries (SCIs) by synthesizing prior literature and incorporating emerging industry trends. It examines the mechanisms and factors that shape whether and how intermediaries create and destroy value in the supply chain, and organizes the existing literature around open questions about how intermediaries' compensation and practices affect supply chain outcomes across different settings. The framework addresses key questions about SCIs' role and motivates future research that can generate actionable insights for managers and policymakers.
