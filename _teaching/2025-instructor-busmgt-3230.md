Instructor of record for BUSMGT 3230, Introduction to Operations Management. Average score on the Student Evaluation of Instructor (SEI): **4.8 out of 5.0**.

My teaching philosophy is to reduce complexity by making ideas clear and relatable. In this course, I anchored every lecture to the Input &rarr; Process &rarr; Output framework, connected it to real-world applications (from airport service operations to manufacturing supply chains), and used end-of-course Kahoot quizzes with small prizes to keep engagement high through the second half of the semester.

Selected student comments:

> "Professor thoroughly explained all topics and math involved in work. He always welcomed questions and would answer them thoroughly."

> "The professor used outside examples such as videos to help deepen understanding and use real world experience for the material... The Kahoot quizzes were also fun and having the top 3 win stress balls was a fun challenge and helped me pay attention in class."

> "I think that the review sessions after exams helped me substantially in my understanding of the material, as well as the visual representation of concepts on the whiteboard."

> "He was very passionate about what he is teaching and truly cares about his students. Having a professor like this makes me want to learn."
